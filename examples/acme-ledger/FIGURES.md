## How it works (design facts)

- How it works: seven steps, one gate. Acme Ledger runs seven steps (Read, Match, Explain, Plan, Approve, Post,
  Report) with one approval gate. Source: product guide (fictional).
- The first four steps only read and change nothing; there is one approval gate (Approve) before anything posts.
  Source: product guide (fictional).

## Pilot results

- The pilot ran in 2 business units for 3 month-end closes. Source: pilot plan (fictional).
- Median close time in the pilot was 3 working days (shown on the slide as a 3 days median close). Source: pilot
  close log (fictional).

  evidence; 1,117 (9%) were left as exceptions for people, which is about 1 line in 11. Source: run log, October,
  North (fictional).
- 0 entries were posted without a named approver during the pilot (0 unapproved postings). Source: posting audit
  log (fictional).
- 14 times the controller declined a posting plan at review: the controller declined 14 posting plans at review
  during the pilot, and each plan was fixed before posting. Source: approvals log (fictional).
