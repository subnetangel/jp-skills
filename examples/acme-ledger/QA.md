# Likely questions: Acme Ledger leadership briefing

Hard ones first. Answer in one or two sentences, then stop. ⚠ marks an answer that needs a fact the speaker must
confirm before the meeting; until then, give the holding line and follow up in writing. Every number here is in
`FIGURES.md`.

1. **Can it post anything without us?**
   "No. Planning only reads. It posts only an approved plan, and the approver's name goes on the batch."

2. **What if the plan is wrong?**
   "Then the controller declines it. That happened 14 times in the pilot, and each one was fixed before anything
   posted."

3. **Is three days what we'd see everywhere?**
   "It's the pilot median across two units and three closes. The next quarter is how we find out for more units."

4. **⚠ What will it cost?**
   "We didn't measure cost per close in the pilot. I'll bring an estimate to the sponsor in week one."
   Open item: the speaker needs a cost estimate with a source before promising a date for it.

5. **⚠ Does it work for our bigger entities?**
   "We haven't tested entities with more than three bank accounts. I'd put one in the next phase to find out."
   Open item: confirm which entity could join the next phase.

6. **What happens to the lines it can't match?**
   "They go to the close team with a reason attached. In the October close for one entity that was 1,117 lines out
   of 12,412."

7. **⚠ Who approves, and can they approve their own plan?**
   "The controller on duty approves. Who may approve their own work follows our existing approval rule, and I'll
   confirm that in writing."
   Open item: the speaker confirms the approval rule with the controller; this example states no rule as fact.

## If the room goes quiet

"The question I usually get is whether it can post on its own. It can't. Want me to show where the gate is?" Then
go back to slide 3 and click to Approve.
