# Rehearsal plan: Acme Ledger leadership briefing

Three drills, then one timed run. Do them in this order, out loud, standing.

| Drill | What you do | What good sounds like |
|---|---|---|
| 1. The 60-second version | Say only the one-line idea, the 8 days, the gate line, the 3 days and the ask. | A colleague can repeat the message and the ask back to you, in their own words. |
| 2. The gate line, three times | Slide 3's Approve paragraph, out loud. | A real two-second stop before "Then it stops", a slower pace after it, your eyes on one person. |
| 3. The hardest question, cold | Someone asks "What will it cost?" without warning. | The ⚠ holding answer in under 15 seconds, with no number you can't source. |
| 4. One timed run | The whole talk with a clock and the clicks, from the presenter build. | Into questions by 6:00, no line read off the slide, both room moments either done or cut on purpose. |

## What to listen for

- **Pace.** The plan is 120 words a minute. If the timed run lands much earlier than 6:00, you are rushing the
  pauses; if much later, cut the optional story first, then the room moments. Never plan to talk faster.
- **The anchor lines.** The six underlined lines in the talk track should come out word for word. Everything else
  can be your own words.
- **The ask.** Say it once, then stop. The silence after "Which unit would you put in first?" is part of the talk.

## Recall check

After the run, ask one listener: "What was the one thing, and what did I ask for?" If they can't say both, the
opening or the close needs work, not the middle.

## Delivery cues used (sparingly)

Pause, slow and softer cues mark only the moments where they change meaning: the 8-day number, the gate, the 14
declines, the ask. The two room moments are optional and cost about 8 seconds each.
