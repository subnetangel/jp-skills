# Craft for Agents: Instagram Reels research and production evidence

Verified 2026-11-20. Public sources only. Platform documentation explains mechanics, not a formula for virality. Case numbers are snapshots with their original metric and scope; never substitute views for unique reach or bookings.

## Evidence hierarchy

- **Organic ranking/discovery:** [Instagram's ranking explainer by Adam Mosseri, May 2023](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained) ([original Instagram release syndicated in full](https://www.webwire.com/ViewPressRel.asp?aId=305828), direct fetch rate-limited). Reels ranking predicts behavior including watching through, resharing and liking, informed by viewer behavior, the video's audio/visuals and creator information. Low-resolution, watermarked, muted, bordered and mostly text Reels can be less visible. Treat this as a historical official description, not current exact weights. [Meta Q3 2026 prepared remarks](https://s21.q4cdn.com/399680738/files/content_files/META-Q3-2026-Earnings-Call-Transcript.pdf) describe richer watch-time signals and sequence ranking; fixed weights, thresholds and guarantees are not disclosed. Check recommendation eligibility and recent account-specific guidance in [Instagram's professional dashboard Best Practices](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/10/best-practices-education-hub-creators-instagram/). [Meta Q4 2024 prepared remarks](https://s21.q4cdn.com/399680738/files/doc_financials/2024/q4/META-Q4-2024-Prepared-Remarks.pdf) confirm prioritizing originals for recommendations. Build from owned/cleared source files and export clean platform-specific masters.
- **Experiments:** [Meta Trial Reels](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/12/trial-reels-try-content-non-followers-first-see-what-perfoms-best/) reach non-followers first, with occasional follower exposure through shares or discovery. Early metrics are available after about 24 hours. Optional automatic release uses performance in the first 72 hours. Leave it off when a human has reserved publication approval; Trial itself publishes to real people. Compare trials to trials. [Meta's June 2025 rollout update](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/06/inspiring-creativity-that-brings-people-together/) says 40% of creators posted Reels more often after trying Trial Reels; 80% **of that subset** increased non-follower reach. Vendor-reported association, not causal lift or an 80% probability for any post.
- **Measurement:** [Meta's Reels metrics explanation](https://about.fb.com/news/2023/04/instagram-reels-trending-audio-and-gifts-updates/) says total watch time includes replays and describes average watch time. Record exactly what the current UI offers and preserve denominators, exposure windows and paid status. Edits or native analytics may expose moment-level skip/retention; do not fabricate missing dimensions.
- **Paid evidence only:** [Meta Reels Ads Guide, Meta-authored PDF mirrored by SavaBien](https://usercontent.one/wp/www.savabien.be/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Reels-Ads-Guide-052924a.pdf?media=1736702060), pp. 3–4 and notes 10–12: 15 Reels-only split tests found an average 34.5% lower cost per result for the bundle of 9:16 video, audio and safe-zone positioning vs still images, reported 99.9% confidence. Separate 15-test comparison against smaller-ratio silent video reported 15% lower CPR at only 70% confidence. Exact split-test dates are not stated there. Variables were bundled; neither result measures organic reach, a single editing choice, or a travel-category guarantee. Use audio and safe-zone preview as robust production defaults, not ranking hacks. Its conservative paid template reserves the bottom 35% for UI. Organic UI varies, so check current live previews rather than claiming a universal pixel-safe zone. Music cleared for Instagram may not be cleared for ads, export or other networks; verify per use, or use owned/explicitly licensed music and production audio.

## Case ledger: real outcomes with limits

| Case and firsthand evidence | Outcome and window | Paid status, confounds, takeaway |
|---|---|---|
| [Rune Gibril first Reel, Project Monet operator report with dated Insights](https://www.projectmonet.com/results/rune-gibril/first-reel) | Published Oct 21, 2026; 334,944 views and 270,012 reached at ~24 h; Oct 31 snapshot 1,821,944 views, 1,512,947 viewers and 1,309 Reel-attributed follows. | Operator says zero spend. New profile, prelaunch positioning, recognizable Mary Oliver poem and audience fit all changed together. Selected success, not a randomized test. Test an immediately recognizable subject aligned with the audience you want; do not reproduce copyrighted material without permission. |
| [Rune Gibril Toni Morrison Trial, same operator](https://www.projectmonet.com/results/rune-gibril/trial-reels) | Oct 31, 2026 screenshot: 833,235 views, 584,612 viewers, 315 attributed follows and 12 s average watch time. | Operator says organic. Lifetime snapshot includes later general sharing; no trial-only capture, exact promotion time or complete losing variants. Illustrates testing and graduation, not proof Trial caused the outcome. Both cases share operator/account and are not independent replications. |
| [Walking Post Thames reopening, creator/producer writeup](https://www.lucymaddison.com/1-million-views-later-what-we-learned-from-going-viral-on-instagram/) | Posted same day a path link opened; two 50K+ accounts shared within a day. Author reports >1M views, 26K likes, >500 comments and ~12K new followers by writeup; exact capture date/window and attribution not stated. | Described as share-led; paid status not explicitly documented. Existing community, timely genuine news and larger accounts' amplification are strong confounds. Handheld phone and simple edits sufficed; show a genuinely new local experience and explain why someone should save/share it. Web traffic, signups and donations reported directionally without counts or attribution design. |
| [Visit Haines bear footage, official borough report, March 6 2025](https://www.hainesalaska.gov/media/88546) | Sep 12, 2024 posts; IG dashboard 90,532 views, 56,218 unique reach, 3,394 interactions, 94% non-follower viewers; curve through day 7. Separate Facebook Reel reached 491,340, different metric/window. | Borough describes organic breakout. Report calls IG item a post and explicitly calls FB item a Reel; don't mislabel proof as IG-Reels-specific. Wildlife novelty, destination affinity, creator attribution and cross-platform distribution confound. Useful adjacent tourism proof, not a reason to stage risky wildlife interactions. |
| [Navi creator + Reels ads, advertiser quote in Meta India Newsroom](https://about.fb.com/news/2023/05/helping-brands-in-india-supercharge-their-growth-with-reels-ads/amp/) | Reported 8% reduction in cost per first-time transaction in early-2023 initiative; exact brand campaign dates, spend and baseline method not disclosed. | Paid. Creator identity, campaign settings/placement and creative are bundled; no organic benchmark. Separate awareness and acquisition goals and use a rate/CPA with the defined conversion event. |

Reject insufficiently dated agency wins as outcome benchmarks; creator/agency dashboards are evidence about their result, not independently audited population data. A cadence, sound, hook or duration found in a winning case is a hypothesis, not a cause. In particular no universal “best 7 seconds,” guaranteed view threshold, mandatory trending sound or fixed cut count.

For technical requirements use the current [Instagram Platform content-publishing specification](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/instagram-platform/content-publishing) for API delivery, and the app or Ads Manager requirements for the actual placement. An API requirement is not necessarily a native app requirement.

## Secondary adaptation

- [TikTok for Business Creative Codes](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en-US/creative-codes): paid-platform guidance supports vertical, native hook/body/close, sound and motion. Recut the clean master for TikTok pacing, text and UI; check commercial music license separately. Do not take paid platform benchmarks as organic Instagram evidence.
- [YouTube Shorts official guide](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10059070): since Mar 31, 2025, views count starts/replays without a minimum watch time; compare engaged views and retention for old/new Shorts when appropriate. Reframe title/first seconds for YouTube audience and check music/rights. A single cross-network view total is not a standardized reach measurement.
