How it works

Seven steps. Nothing changes until a person says yes.

Read-onlyHuman approvalMakes changes

Step 1 · read-only

Discover

Finds every cluster the notice covers, across all your environments.

Step 2 · read-only

Analyze

Checks versions, extensions and the breaking changes that apply to you.

Step 3 · read-only

Plan

Writes a plan file: the order, the windows and the rollback for each cluster.

Step 4 · read-only

Dry run

Rehearses the plan on a copy and reports what would happen.

Step 5 · human gate

Approve

A named person reviews the plan and says yes. Until then, nothing changes.

Step 6 · makes changes

Execute

Runs the approved plan in your pipeline, one window at a time.

Step 7 · read-only

Validate

Checks health after each change and rolls back if a check fails.
