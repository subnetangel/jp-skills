How it works

Five steps. Nothing reaches the books before step 3.

  1. MatchEach bank line to an invoice or receipt, with the evidence attached.
  2. PlanA posting plan, plus every exception with its reason.
  3. ApproveThe controller on duty reads the plan and says yes or no.
  4. PostOnly the approved plan, with the approver's name on the batch.
  5. ReportA close report your auditors can read.
