# Figures behind the sample demo

Every number on a slide, in the session or in the report traces to a line here. All of it is fictional sample data
for the fictional product Acme Ledger. Replace this file with your own sources when you make the demo yours.

- Month-end close took 8 working days on average over the last six closes before the pilot. Source: finance
  operations close log (fictional).
- The pilot ran in 2 business units for 3 month-end closes. Source: pilot plan (fictional).
- Median close time in the pilot was 3 working days. Source: pilot close log (fictional).
- The October close for the North entity read 12,412 bank lines from 3 bank accounts and 9,870 open invoices and
  receipts. Source: run log, October, North (fictional).
- 11,295 bank lines (91%) were matched with evidence; 1,117 (9%) were left as exceptions. Source: run log, October,
  North (fictional).
- Exception groups: 604 with no matching invoice yet, 331 where the amount differs by a fee, 2 where two lines point
  at one invoice, 180 other. Source: close report, October, North (fictional).
- 0 entries were posted without a named approver during the pilot. Source: posting audit log (fictional).
- 14 posting plans were declined at review during the pilot and fixed before posting. Source: approvals log
  (fictional).
- Proposed next step (a proposal, not a result): 2 more business units for the next quarter, with 1 controller as
  sponsor.
